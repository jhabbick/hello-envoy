The workflow

How a wedding runs in Envoy

  1. 1

    Describe the wedding

    Guest count, date or season, location, and the vendors you need — specificity gets better matches.

  2. 2

    Shortlist contacts

    Envoy groups providers by category. You choose who to contact. No post-and-wait lead blast.

  3. 3

    Create the project

    Title, description, and location in one hub. Add budget and deadline when you have them.

  4. 4

    Plan in Chat

    Refine scope, timeline, and priorities. When you're ready, Envoy drafts outreach from the project.

  5. 5

    Approve & send

    Review every message, revise if needed, then send from your connected Gmail or Outlook. Threads and replies stay with you.
